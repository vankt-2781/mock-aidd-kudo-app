# Business Context — HomepageSaa

## Why It Matters

The SAA 2025 homepage is the public front door of the event campaign. It builds anticipation (countdown to the event), explains the "Root Further" theme, and sends people to the award categories and the Kudos recognition program. Without it, the app opens on starter boilerplate and visitors have no way to learn about the event.

## Who Uses It

- **Visitor (not signed in)** — Browses the event story, countdown, award categories and Kudos promo, and follows links to the detail pages. Needs no account.
- **Signed-in employee** — Sees the same page plus a notification bell and an account menu, and can sign out from it.
- **Admin** — A signed-in employee whose account is marked as admin by the team; additionally sees an Admin Dashboard entry in the account menu.
- **Vietnamese and English readers** — Switch the page language between VN and EN.

## What They Do

1. A visitor opens the homepage — sees the theme, the event date and place, and the time left until the event starts.
2. The visitor reads the Root Further story and the six award categories — understands what is being recognized.
3. The visitor selects an award card — arrives at the Awards page scrolled to that category.
4. The visitor selects the Kudos promo or the Kudos button — arrives at the Kudos page.
5. The visitor switches the language — the whole page changes between Vietnamese and English.
6. A signed-in employee opens the account menu — chooses Profile or signs out; an admin may also open the Admin Dashboard.
7. If the event start is not configured correctly — the countdown shows zeros and the "Coming soon" label is hidden, so the page never looks broken.
